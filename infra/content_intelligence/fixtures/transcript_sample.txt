A tiny puppy was trapped under a storm drain in the middle of a busy street.
At first everyone just walked past, but then a delivery driver stopped his truck,
blocked traffic, and started prying the grate open with a crowbar.
The puppy kept crying and the crowd started filming.
Two strangers jumped in to help, and after about three minutes they finally
lifted the grate and pulled the puppy out.
The puppy was shaking but okay, and the driver wrapped it in his jacket.
The whole street erupted in cheers, and someone started crying.
